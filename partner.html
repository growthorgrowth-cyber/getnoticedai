Partner FAQ

How much do I earn per referral?
You earn 20% of every monthly payment your referrals make. Growth plan referrals ($497/mo) earn you $99.40/mo. Authority plan referrals ($697/mo) earn you $139.40/mo. There is no cap — refer as many businesses as you want.
When and how do I get paid?
Payouts are sent monthly, on the 15th of each month, for the previous month's commissions. We pay via PayPal, Venmo, Zelle, or direct deposit — whichever you prefer. You'll receive a payout summary email each month showing exactly which referrals generated your commission.
How does referral tracking work?
You'll receive a unique referral link (e.g. getnoticedai.com?ref=yourname). When someone clicks your link and signs up, the referral is automatically attributed to you. Tracking is cookie-based and lasts 90 days — so even if they don't sign up immediately, you still get credit if they return within 90 days.
Is there a minimum number of referrals required?
No. There is no minimum. You can refer one business and earn commission on that one account indefinitely. There is also no maximum — the program scales with you.
What happens if a referral cancels?
Your commission is tied to active subscriptions. If a referral cancels, their commission stops at the end of their billing period. If they re-subscribe later, the commission resumes.
Can I refer my own clients if I'm an agency?
Yes — this is actually one of the best use cases. If you manage marketing for local businesses, you can refer your clients and earn a recurring commission on top of your existing retainer. Many of our partners are marketing agencies and consultants.
How long does it take to get approved?
We review applications within 1 business day. Once approved, you'll receive your unique referral link and access to your partner dashboard via email.
