Partner FAQ

How much do I earn per referral?
Growth plan referrals earn 20% recurring ($99.40 per monthly payment), and Authority plan referrals earn 20% recurring ($139.40 per monthly payment), while the referred subscription remains active. Command referrals earn a $1,000 flat fee after the referred client’s implementation payment is received. Command does not pay recurring commission.
When and how do I get paid?
Growth and Authority commissions are calculated from active subscription payments and paid on the normal monthly partner payout schedule. The Command referral fee becomes payable only after the referred client’s implementation payment is received. We pay via PayPal, Venmo, Zelle, or direct deposit and provide a payout summary.
How does referral tracking work?
You'll receive a unique referral link (e.g. getnoticedai.com?ref=yourname). When someone clicks your link and signs up, the referral is automatically attributed to you. Tracking is cookie-based and lasts 90 days — so even if they don't sign up immediately, you still get credit if they return within 90 days.
Is there a minimum number of referrals required?
No. There is no minimum. One active Growth or Authority referral can generate recurring commission, and one qualifying Command referral can generate the $1,000 flat fee after paid implementation.
What happens if a referral cancels?
For Growth and Authority, recurring commission is tied to an active paid subscription and stops when that subscription ends. Command is a one-time referral fee and is earned only after the implementation payment is received.
Can I refer my own clients if I'm an agency?
Yes. Agencies and consultants can earn 20% recurring on eligible Growth and Authority subscriptions, or a $1,000 flat fee for a Command referral after paid implementation.
How long does it take to get approved?
We review applications within 1 business day. Once approved, you'll receive your unique referral link and access to your partner dashboard via email.
