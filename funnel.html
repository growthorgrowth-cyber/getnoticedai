100% set-it-and-forget-it. The setup takes about 10 minutes. You connect your Google account, answer a few basic questions about your business, and the AI takes over. You never have to log in again unless you want to check your monthly reports.

Yes. If you have multiple locations, you can manage them all. Each location requires its own subscription plan, and you can connect multiple Google accounts if your profiles are spread across different accounts.

Yes. AI search engines pull their local business data from major directories and Google Maps. By optimizing your profile and syncing your citations (Authority plan), you significantly increase your chances of being recommended by ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, and others.
