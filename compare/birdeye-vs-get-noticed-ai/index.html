Birdeye vs Get Noticed AI: Which Model Fits Your Business?

Birdeye is a broad, established platform built around multi-location visibility, reviews, messaging, and customer experience. Get Noticed AI gives local businesses two narrower paths: Nova for automated local visibility and Command for managed follow-up and booking operations.

Start with the model

These products overlap, but they are not organized around the same buyer.

Birdeye’s current product family spans listings, reviews, messaging, social, search visibility, automation, insights, surveys, and customer experience. Its public positioning centers on multi-location brands and AI agents that work across locations. That breadth can be valuable when a larger organization wants a consolidated platform.

Get Noticed AI is intentionally more focused. Nova automates the recurring local-visibility work smaller businesses commonly neglect. Command is a separate, higher-touch offer for established businesses that want Get Noticed AI to install and manage lead response, booking, follow-up, review, and local-visibility workflows.

The practical decision
  • Choose for the operating model you want—not the longest feature list.
  • Separate local visibility from lead conversion before comparing tools.
  • Decide whether your team wants software access or managed ownership.

A broad reputation and visibility platform with real multi-location depth.

Birdeye’s official product pages document centralized listings and Google Business Profile work, review generation and response, customer messaging, unified inboxes, contact management, local reporting, and AI-search visibility. Birdeye also emphasizes location-level controls, reporting, and brand consistency.

Where Get Noticed AI differs

Two focused routes: automate the visibility layer or hand off the revenue workflow.

Nova: local visibility automation

Nova is for a local business that wants the recurring Google Business Profile, review, citation, posting, and visibility work to run with less manual effort. It is the lower-cost, productized route.

Best fit

Birdeye may be the better fit when breadth and multi-location control lead the decision.

  • You manage many locations and need location-level governance.
  • You want listings, reviews, messaging, social, search visibility, surveys, and insights under a broad platform.
  • Your team has the capacity to administer a sophisticated software environment and vendor implementation.
Do not choose by assumption.

Ask Birdeye for a current package, implementation scope, integrations list, service level, and total price for your location count. Compare that written scope with the exact Nova or Command outcome you need.

When Get Noticed AI may fit

Choose the narrower path when your problem is clear.

Use Nova when the recurring local-visibility work needs to run without adding another internal marketing role. Consider Command when the bigger issue is operational: leads arrive, but response, booking, follow-up, reviews, and accountability still depend on people remembering.

FAQs

Questions local businesses ask before switching.

Is Get Noticed AI a direct replacement for Birdeye?
Not in every use case. Birdeye offers a broad multi-location platform across listings, reviews, messaging, social, search, and customer experience. Nova is narrower local-visibility automation, while Command is a managed operating layer. Compare the exact outcomes and integrations you need.
Does Birdeye support Google Business Profile and listings?
Yes. Birdeye’s current Listings AI pages describe Google Business Profile optimization, directory management, duplicate suppression, local keyword recommendations, and analytics. This page does not claim otherwise.
Does Birdeye offer messaging and AI lead response?
Yes. Birdeye’s current Messaging AI materials describe instant AI responses, lead qualification, omnichannel conversations, unified inboxes, contact management, and trigger-based automation.
When should I look at Nova instead?
Look at Nova when the primary need is a focused, productized local-visibility system rather than a broad enterprise-style platform. Review the current Nova plan scope against your listings, reviews, posting, and reporting requirements.
When should I look at Command?
Look at Command when leads are already coming in but response, booking, follow-up, review generation, and accountability remain inconsistent. Command is application-only and managed to a defined scope.
How should I verify pricing?
Request current written pricing and scope directly from each provider. Birdeye packages are customized by location and solution mix. Get Noticed publishes Nova options; Command’s standard scope starts at $5,000 implementation and $3,500 per month.
