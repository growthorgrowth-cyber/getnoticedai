Podium is an established AI lead-management and customer-communication platform. Get Noticed AI separates the decision into Nova for local visibility automation and Command for a managed response, booking, follow-up, review, and visibility operating system.

Start with the operating model

Podium brings communication tools together. Get Noticed AI asks who will own the outcome.

Podium’s current public materials center on an AI Employee, a unified inbox, phone and text communication, lead response, appointment booking, reviews, payments, contact records, reporting, and database re-engagement. That makes Podium a credible all-in-one communications and conversion platform for local businesses.

Get Noticed AI does not claim Podium lacks those capabilities. The key distinction is scope and ownership. Nova handles a focused local-visibility layer. Command is a managed service in which Get Noticed AI installs and manages the defined lead-response and booking system instead of asking the owner to turn software into an operating process alone.

The deciding question

Do you primarily need a capable communication platform for your team to run, a focused local-visibility automation, or an outside operator to own a defined response-and-follow-up system?

Where Podium is strong

A mature communication and conversion platform with broad local-business functionality.

Podium’s official pages document 24/7 AI lead response, qualification and booking, phones, automatic missed-call texts, AI call summaries, inboxes, web chat, social messages, reviews, text marketing, contact history, reporting, payments, and customer re-engagement.

Best fit

Podium may fit when your team wants one communication platform and will actively administer it.

  • You want phones, texting, web chat, reviews, payments, contact history, and AI lead response in one platform.
  • Your team will own configuration, daily exception handling, user adoption, reporting, and ongoing optimization.
  • Integrated payment collection or a communication inbox is central to the purchase.
Get the written scope.

Ask Podium which AI, phone, inbox, review, payment, automation, and integration features are included in the quoted package. Ask Get Noticed AI the same question for the selected Nova plan or Command implementation.

The owner-dependence test

Software helps only when someone owns the system after launch.

A broad platform can consolidate work and automate conversations. It can still underperform if setup, routing, calendars, exceptions, staff handoffs, data hygiene, and follow-up logic remain unowned. Command is designed for businesses that want that defined operating layer managed for them.

FAQs

Questions local businesses ask before changing platforms.

Is Get Noticed AI a direct replacement for Podium?
Not for every use case. Podium offers phones, messaging, an inbox, reviews, payments, contact history, and AI lead handling. Nova is narrower local-visibility automation. Command is a managed system that may overlap with lead response and follow-up but is not presented as a feature-for-feature Podium clone.
Does Podium handle missed calls and automated response?
Yes. Podium’s official materials describe automatic missed-call texts, AI phone handling, lead response, call summaries, and conversations managed from its inbox.
Does Podium offer reviews and payments?
Yes. Podium documents review invitations, reminders and AI responses, plus text-to-pay, card readers, tap-to-pay, recurring payments, financing options, and payment reporting.
When should I look at Nova?
Look at Nova when the primary need is recurring Google Business Profile, review, citation, posting, and local-visibility work—not a replacement for a comprehensive communications and payments suite.
When should I look at Command?
Look at Command when the business already generates inquiries but response, booking, follow-up, reviews, and visibility still depend on inconsistent manual ownership. Command is application-only and managed to a written scope.
How should I compare pricing?
Compare total written scope rather than a headline rate. Confirm locations, users, AI, phone, text, payment, onboarding, support, integrations, implementation, and ongoing management. Get Noticed’s standard Command price is $5,000 implementation plus $3,500 per month; Nova pricing is published on the homepage.
