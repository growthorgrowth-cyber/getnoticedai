Why It Matters

Citations: a local trust signal worth keeping consistent

A citation is a mention of your business name, address, and phone number on another website or directory. Keeping those details consistent helps customers and search systems find the same business information wherever they look.

Why citations support local trust

Inconsistent or missing business details can create friction for customers and local-search systems. Citation management helps you keep key business information clear, current, and easier to verify across important sources.

  • ✓ Confirms your name, address & phone number are accurate everywhere
  • ✓ Keeps key data aggregators aligned with your profile
  • ✓ Supports the consistent public information AI tools may reference
  • ✓ Duplicate listing detection & cleanup included
